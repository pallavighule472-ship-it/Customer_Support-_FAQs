Welcome to Joe's Cafe

Joe's Cafe is a family-owned Italian restaurant serving authentic cuisine since 1995. We are located in the heart of London and pride ourselves on fresh, homemade food.

Opening Hours

We are open Monday to Friday from 9am to 10pm. On weekends, we open at 11am and close at 9pm. We are closed on Christmas Day and New Year's Day.

Location

We are located at 123 Main Street, Covent Garden, London, WC2N 5DU. We are a 5-minute walk from Covent Garden tube station on the Piccadilly line.

Contact Us

You can reach us by phone at 020-1234-5678 or by email at hello@joescafe.co.uk. We typically respond to emails within 24 hours.

Menu & Cuisine

We specialize in authentic Italian cuisine including handmade pasta, wood-fired pizzas, and classic desserts like tiramisu and panna cotta. We also offer a selection of Italian wines and cocktails.

Our menu changes seasonally to reflect the freshest ingredients available. A full menu is available on request or at the restaurant.

Dietary Options

We cater for vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free diets. Please inform your server of any allergies or dietary requirements when ordering. We take allergies very seriously and our kitchen staff are fully trained.

Reservations

Reservations are recommended, especially on weekends. You can book a table online through our website, by phone at 020-1234-5678, or by emailing reservations@joescafe.co.uk. Walk-ins are welcome subject to availability.

For groups of 6 or more, a reservation is required and a deposit of 10 pounds per person may be requested.

Delivery & Takeaway

We offer takeaway services during all opening hours. Delivery is available through Deliveroo and Uber Eats within a 3-mile radius. Delivery orders can also be placed by calling us directly for orders over 30 pounds.

Parking

There is no dedicated parking at the restaurant. The nearest public car park is at Trafalgar Square, approximately 10 minutes walk away. We recommend using public transport or cycling.

Private Dining & Events

We have a private dining room available for parties of up to 30 guests. We host birthday celebrations, corporate events, and family gatherings. Please contact us at events@joescafe.co.uk for availability and pricing.

Loyalty Programme

Join our Joe's Rewards programme and earn points on every visit. After 10 visits you receive a free dessert. Sign up in-store or on our website. Points cannot be transferred or exchanged for cash.

Gift Vouchers

Gift vouchers are available in denominations of 10, 25, and 50 pounds. They can be purchased in-store or online and are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase. Vouchers are non-refundable.

Wi-Fi

Free Wi-Fi is available for all customers. Ask your server for the password. Our Wi-Fi network is called JoesCafe_Guest.

Accessibility

Joe's Cafe is fully wheelchair accessible with step-free entry and accessible toilets on the ground floor. We also offer large-print menus on request. Guide dogs and assistance animals are welcome.
